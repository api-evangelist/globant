# Glob.AI llms.txt

## About
[Glob.AI](https://glob.ai): Glob.AI is the transformation of the professional services model toward AI-native delivery. It moves away from headcount-based models to focus on "Workflows That Think" and "Intelligent Native Delivery."

## Core Value Proposition
Glob.AI is not a layer on top of legacy systems; it is a system designed from the ground up to operate at the speed of intelligence. It solves the industry gap where speed improved but value remained stagnant due to traditional delivery bottlenecks.

## The AI Pods Model
Services are delivered through specialized AI Pods, combining frontier agentic AI with Globant's industry expertise.

- [AI Pod - Software](https://glob.ai): These pods leverage AI-driven workflows to build and evolve technology from the ground up. By generating production-ready code, architecture designs, and data pipelines under the expert supervision of Globant specialists, they deliver tangible working software artifacts. The client maintains full ownership of the technology, while the pod provides the supervised intelligence needed to accelerate development.
- [AI Pod - Ops](https://glob.ai): Focused on operational efficiency, these pods design, deploy, and manage AI-powered workflows that automate complex business processes from start to finish. They are built to ensure continuous performance maximization through a supervised runtime model, allowing companies to scale their operations with precision and speed without increasing headcount.
- [AI Pod - Catalyst](https://glob.ai): The Catalyst model provides a pure self-service SaaS infrastructure for organizations that want complete control. It offers the tools and environment necessary to build, deploy, and manage proprietary AI workflows independently. With no external supervision required, it operates on a consumption-based model, offering maximum flexibility and speed for internal teams.

## Technical Architecture: The 4 Hubs
Glob.AI operates through a security-first architecture divided into four functional hubs.
- AI Hub: Universal routing and connection to 140+ LLMs with built-in governance.
- Corporate Hub: Secure integration with all internal corporate systems and data.
- Agent Hub: Creation and management of specialized agents combining company data with LLM logic.
- AI Pods Hub: The execution layer for supervised and automated workflows.

## The Token Vault: Institutional Knowledge
The Token Vault is a secure hub for storing every interaction and decision.
- Knowledge Compounding: Unlike traditional consulting, where knowledge leaves with the person, Glob.AI encodes institutional knowledge into the platform.
- Data Sovereignty: Customers maintain full ownership of their proprietary logic and tokens.
- Continuous Improvement: The system becomes more valuable over time as it learns from the company's specific domain.

## The Engines
- Value-Based Subscriptions: Payment for outcomes and outputs (supervised tokens), not hours.
- Agent Marketplace: A repository of open-source and commercial agents (Salesforce, ServiceNow, etc.) optimized through the platform.
- Infrastructure Pass-Through: Proprietary algorithms for cost-efficient token routing across multiple models.

## Domain Expertise
Every AI Pod is infused with Globant's deep industry knowledge across sectors, including Financial Services, Healthcare, Media, Retail, Sports, Gaming, Airlines, Automotive, and more.

- [How is security and governance handled?](https://glob.ai): Glob.AI is built on a security-first architecture. It includes universal routing with built-in governance, data residency controls, and compliance policies across all models to prevent vendor lock-in and ensure enterprise-grade protection.
- [What is the Agent Marketplace?](https://glob.ai): It is a repository where users can access, manage, and optimize both open-source and commercial agents through the Glob.AI platform.
